# Visa lawyer portal

**For:** registered migration agents and Australian legal practitioners.
**Promise:** complete, well-organised medical treatment visa applications, with everything in one case.
**Demo login:** `lawyer@demo.local` / `Demo1234` (James Whitfield, MARN, Sydney).

## The journey

**Joining:**

1. Sign up with consent and verify email.
2. Complete the 3-step registration: practice with ABN, credential, documents and agreement.
3. Choose a credential: a **MARN** (7 digits, on the OMARA register) or an **Australian practising certificate** with the issuing state. Lawyers with a practising certificate haven't needed a MARN since 2021.
4. MTA checks that register and approves, rejects or asks for more information. No fees are charged yet.

**Each case:**

1. **New:** MTA assigns a submitted application, and the lawyer is notified.
2. **Under Review:** read the form, documents, hospital letters and treatment logistics.
3. **Docs Pending:** request anything missing. The patient is told and uploads it.
4. **Processing:** the application is lodged.
5. **Approved or Rejected:** a rejection needs a reason. The doctor and hospital are told the result.
6. **Visa grant:** upload it and the patient can download it straight away.

## Questions you may get

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can I see other lawyers' cases? | No. Only cases MTA assigned to you; anything else simply doesn't exist for you. |
| Do you check my registration? | Yes. MTA checks the OMARA register or law society and records it. Approval is blocked until it passes. |
| Which visa does this cover? | The medical treatment visa, subclass 602. |
| Can I reject without a reason? | No, a reason is required. |
| Who assigns cases? | MTA, under Visa Cases. |

## Simulated in this demo

Nothing is lodged with Home Affairs. The platform records the lawyer's status updates and doesn't connect to any government system.
