# Hospital portal

**For:** partner hospitals receiving international patients.
**Promise:** see who is coming and when, and issue visa letters in minutes.
**Demo login:** `hospital@demo.local` / `Demo1234` (St Vincent's Hospital Sydney).

## The journey

**Joining:**

1. Sign up and verify email.
2. Complete the 4-step registration: basics with ABN, location, hospital details, documents.
3. MTA checks accreditation and the state licence, then approves, rejects or asks for more information.
4. The hospital becomes selectable by doctors as an affiliation. No fees are charged yet.

**Each case:**

1. **Plan received:** a doctor names the hospital in a treatment plan, and the case appears.
2. **Patient pays:** the hospital is alerted that letters are needed.
3. **Generate letters:** the Hospital Invitation Letter and Appointment Confirmation Letter become PDFs on the hospital's letterhead.
4. **Send to patient:** once both are sent, the patient's visa application unlocks.
5. **Admission:** dates appear under upcoming admissions.

## Questions you may get

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can we edit the case or the plan? | No. Issuing the visa letters is the hospital's only action on a case. |
| Why can't we see a patient's phone number? | Patients are shown with clinical details only, by design. |
| Can we add or remove doctors? | Not here. Doctors choose their affiliations in their own settings. |
| What if a letter has an error? | Regenerate it. The new version replaces the old one and the sent status stays. |
| Do letters support Chinese names? | Not yet: characters outside the Latin alphabet print as "?". Use the Latin spelling on the passport. |
