# Doctor portal

**For:** Australian specialists treating international patients.
**Promise:** qualified referrals, with the paperwork handled and the return on membership visible.
**Demo login:** `doctor@demo.local` / `Demo1234` (Dr. Amelia Chen, Sydney).

## The journey

**Joining:**

1. Sign up and verify email.
2. Complete the 4-step registration: details, qualifications and AHPRA number, hospitals and availability, documents.
3. MTA checks the AHPRA register and approves, rejects or asks for more information.
4. Pay the one-time registration fee (AUD 5,000 by default; annual renewal AUD 2,000).
5. Declare professional indemnity, which is required before offering consultation times.

**Each case:**

1. **Invitation:** accept or decline, with a 48-hour response target.
2. **Evaluation:** review history and documents, request more if needed.
3. **Initial Zoom consultation:** offer up to three times in the doctor's own time zone. The patient picks one and pays.
4. **Treatment plan:** diagnosis, procedure, timeline, hospital, costs. Submission stays locked until the consultation has happened or the doctor skips it with a reason.
5. **MTA review:** MTA publishes or declines the plan with a reason.
6. **Patient decision:** several doctors may send plans, and the patient chooses one.
7. **Treatment:** mark as completed once the end date has passed.

## Questions you may get

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who sets my fees? | The doctor enters them in each plan. MTA adds its own service fee when publishing. |
| Can a plan skip the consultation? | Yes, with a reason that MTA can see, for example when they've already spoken. |
| What if the patient picks another doctor? | The case closes for the other doctors and appears under "Why cases didn't proceed". |
| How is my licence checked? | MTA staff check the AHPRA public register and record the result. Approval is blocked until it passes. |
| Can other doctors see my prices? | No. Only anonymised medians are shown. |
