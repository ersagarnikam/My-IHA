# Admin portal

The MTA coordinators' workspace at `/admin` (role `ADMIN`, API under `/api/admin/**`). English only, like the doctor portal. It reuses the doctor portal's styles and helpers (`portals/doctor/doctor.css`, `portals/doctor/shared.tsx`); its pages live in `frontend/src/portals/admin/` and its endpoints in the backend `admin/` package.

## Admin accounts

Nobody can sign up as an admin. On startup `AdminBootstrap` creates the first admin from `IHA_ADMIN_EMAIL` and `IHA_ADMIN_PASSWORD` (at least 12 characters) when no account with that email exists; it never changes an existing account. Leave the variables empty once the account exists. In development the seeder creates `admin@demo.local` / `Demo1234`.

## Pages

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Built only from live records (no estimates), from `GET /api/admin/insights` (`AdminInsightsService`). A teal hero with money received from patients in the last 12 months and MTA's service fees, plus active doctors, patients, cases in progress and treated; the four attention tiles (registrations to review, cases without a doctor, plans to review, waiting conversations — also shown as sidebar badges); the **case pipeline** by stage; **money by month** (patient payments and doctor membership fees); a **Doctors** table (active cases, unanswered invitations, acceptance rate, average response time, plans sent, share of plans patients chose, patients treated, doctor fees earned, membership status, last activity) with counts by registration status; **Needs a nudge** (invitations unanswered for 48 hours, registration fees unpaid, annual fees overdue or due within 30 days, and **compliance**: AHPRA, accreditation and licence checks missing, failed, expired or ending within 30 days, a hospital's declared expiry dates, and active doctors whose indemnity declaration is missing or lapsing — `verification/ComplianceService`); **upcoming Zoom consultations** (next two weeks); **specialty coverage** (active doctors against open cases, flagging specialties with no doctor); recent cases. |
| Doctor Approvals | Registrations by status (Pending, More Info Requested, Approved, Rejected), searchable. The detail page of an approved doctor starts with **Performance** (`GET /api/admin/doctors/{id}/performance`): the same figures as the dashboard table, membership fees paid, and every case the doctor was invited to with their answer, plan status and case status. It then shows **Register checks** (below), the professional details, the doctor's **indemnity & telehealth declaration** (insurer, policy, expiry, overseas cover; *Not declared* / *Current* / *Expired*) and documents (view/download) with **Approve** (enabled once the AHPRA check passes), **Request more info** and **Reject**; the last two need a reason (≥ 10 characters) that the doctor sees and can answer by resubmitting. Approved doctors then pay the registration fee themselves. |
| Hospital Approvals | Hospitals that registered through the hospital portal, by status, searchable. The review page shows **Register checks** (NSQHS accreditation and state private-hospital licence), the hospital's details (type, registration and licence numbers, ABN, beds, rooms, specialties, address, declared accreditation and licence expiry dates) and documents, with **Approve** (enabled once both checks pass), **Request more info** and **Reject** (reason required for the last two, seen by the hospital). Approval opens the hospital portal and lets doctors pick the hospital as an affiliation. |
| Cases | All cases, with a *Needs a doctor* view. The case page shows the patient's description, documents, invited doctors and their progress (including a skipped initial consultation and its reason), plans and consultations. **Invite a doctor** lists active doctors (approved and fee paid), those with the case's specialty first; inviting moves a submitted case to *under review* and notifies the doctor. |
| Treatment Plans | Plans by status (To Review, With Patient, Chosen & Paid, Declined). The review page shows the full plan and costs. **Approve and send to patient** takes MTA's service fee (suggested 10% of the plan total) and publishes the plan as a treatment option; **Decline** needs a reason, which the doctor sees on the plan. Both notify the doctor. |
| Payments | Every payment received: patients' treatment payments and consultation fees, doctors' registration and annual fees. Tabs (All, Patients, Doctors, With refunds), search by payer, case ID or payment reference, totals received and refunded. Each case page also lists its payments. A payment's page shows what was paid, refunded (before and after card fees), still refundable, the card processing fee and every refund. **Refund** opens a dialog: full or partial amount, a reason (patient changed their mind, visa refused, doctor cancelled, treatment not possible, service not delivered, duplicate, goodwill, other — *other* needs a note), an optional internal note and, for reasons where the fee is kept, *Waive the card processing fee*; it shows the refund, the fee kept and what goes back to the card before you confirm. |
| Settings | **Business details & GST**: MTA's legal name, ABN, address and billing email (shown on every invoice and credit note), *Registered for GST*, and whether each kind of charge is taxable (defaults: MTA service fee and doctor membership fees taxable; treatment costs and consultation fees GST-free — for MTA's accountant to confirm). Registered with an ABN, invoices become **tax invoices** (GST per line, "GST-free" lines, "Total includes GST of") and credit notes **adjustment notes** (the refund's GST in proportion to the payment's); otherwise they say "No GST has been charged." **Platform fees**, grouped by who pays: doctors (registration/onboarding fee, annual membership fee), visa lawyers and hospitals (registration and annual fees — stored now, charged once their portals launch, so they start at $0 and are marked *Not charged yet*), and patients (online consultation fee, suggested MTA service fee %). Edit a value, optionally give a reason, Save. Each fee shows who changed it last; a **change history** lists every change (from, to, who, when, reason). A change applies to payments started afterwards; payments already started keep their amount, and a paid consultation keeps showing what was charged. |
| Messages | One inbox for patient and provider (doctor, hospital, lawyer) support chats: unread counts, reopen requests, **Open / Close conversation**, replies. Admins don't get live pushes yet; the inbox refreshes every 20 seconds. |

## Rules enforced by the API

- Only `pending` registrations can be reviewed.
- Only active doctors can be invited (`doctor_not_active`), and a doctor can't be invited to the same case twice.
- Only submitted plans can be published or declined; the service fee must be ≥ 0. A declined plan's reason is stored on `treatment_plans.rejection_reason` (migration V6).
- Fees: 0 or more with at most 2 decimals, at most AUD 1,000,000 or 100%; fees charged by card today (doctor fees, consultation fee) must be more than 0. Unknown fee keys answer 404.
- Refunds: any admin can refund. The total refunded never exceeds what was paid. The **card processing fee is kept only for reasons on the payer's side** (changed mind, visa refused, goodwill, other — `RefundService.FEE_KEPT`); when the doctor, hospital or MTA is responsible (doctor cancelled, treatment not possible, service not delivered, duplicate) the payer gets everything back and MTA absorbs the fee, in line with Australian Consumer Law (to be confirmed by MTA's lawyer). It is deducted at most once per payment in total (the first refund carries it): with Stripe it is the fee Stripe actually kept on the payment (read from the charge's balance transaction); with the simulated provider it is estimated from the *Card processing fee* settings (default 1.75% + A$0.30 — check them against your Stripe pricing). The admin can waive it when the charge was MTA's mistake; MTA then absorbs it. A refund that would leave nothing after the fee is refused. Money goes back to the original card through the payment provider; a payment refunded in full becomes *Refunded*, otherwise it shows *Partly refunded*.
- What a refund does: the patient (or doctor) gets a notification and email with the amount and any fee kept; patients see the refund in their payment history with a **credit note** PDF, and invoices stay available. A fully refunded consultation that hasn't taken place is cancelled and the doctor is told. A treatment refund doesn't change the case: admin decides what happens next. A refunded doctor fee doesn't change the doctor's access. The dashboard counts money net of refunds, and a fully refunded treatment earns no MTA service fee and no doctor fees.
- Reviews, invitations, plan decisions, fee changes and refunds are written to the audit log with the acting admin.

## Register checks (verification)

AHPRA has no public API, so MTA staff search the [AHPRA public register](https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Registers-of-Practitioners.aspx) (and, for hospitals, the accreditation certificate and the state health department's licence register) and record what they found with **Record check**: *Verified*, *Verified with conditions* (the conditions are required) or *Not verified*, a *valid until* date (required unless not verified) and notes. Each check is kept (`provider_verifications`, audited as `provider.verified`); the latest of each kind is the current one.

- **Approval is blocked** (`409 verification_required`) until every required check is passing and unexpired: AHPRA for doctors; accreditation and licence for hospitals (`verification/VerificationService`).
- Doctors can't offer consultation times (initial or follow-up) until they declare professional indemnity that **covers patients located outside Australia** and acknowledge the Medical Board's telehealth guidelines (`409 indemnity_declaration_required` / `indemnity_expired`).
- Re-check registrations when they renew; lapses show under *Needs a nudge*.
- **For MTA's lawyer to confirm before launch:** the declaration wording, whether overseas cover must be mandatory, and the 30-day warning window.
